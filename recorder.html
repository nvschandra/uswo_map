How to use Conversation Recorder

🎤 Microphone (in-person)

Select Microphone, press Record. Grant mic access when prompted. Works immediately — no setup needed.

🖥 System Audio (meetings)

  1. Select System Audio and press Record.
  2. A screen-share dialog opens — pick any window or the full screen.
  3. Check "Share audio" at the bottom of the dialog before clicking Share.
  4. Transcription starts immediately.

🔀 Both (recommended for meetings you're also speaking in)

Captures your voice via mic and the far end via system audio simultaneously.

💡 Tips

  • Use Chrome or Edge — other browsers don't support live speech recognition.
  • Click Save .txt to export the transcript at any time.
  • System Audio → Share tab audio works best when the meeting is in its own tab.
